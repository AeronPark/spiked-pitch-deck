The Idea

What is SPIKED?

SPIKED is a new category beverage:

  • Sports drink hydration
  • Light energy boost
  • Hard seltzer refreshment
  • Ultra-low alcohol (1%)

It's designed for:

  • Social athletes
  • Fitness & active lifestyle consumers
  • Music festivals & outdoor events
  • Weekend warriors
  • Consumers who want a "buzz" without getting drunk
